## ARD mode note

Under released repo-mode ARD (two-sided, fixed mu0), calibration could not reach 0.5–2 alarms/yr targets (budget_0.5 marked infeasible at h≈128σ). Paper-mode ARD enables feasible calibration for budgets 1 and 2; budget 0.5 often hits the h=64σ ceiling on reduced runs.
